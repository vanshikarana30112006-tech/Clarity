To prevent duplication and inconsistent updates, normalization divides a database into related tables. In 1NF each cell holds an atomic value. In 2NF every non-key column depends on the entire primary key, eliminating partial dependencies. In 3NF a non-key column must not depend on another non-key column, eliminating transitive dependencies. A foreign key can link a student table to a department table. This improves consistency but may require additional joins.
