Stacks remove the newest item first, which is called LIFO. Their push and pop operations work at the top. Queues remove the oldest item first, called FIFO. Enqueue inserts at the back and dequeue removes at the front. These operations can run in O(1) time with a suitable implementation. Undo history uses a stack while a printer waiting list uses a queue. Removing from an empty collection requires an underflow check.
